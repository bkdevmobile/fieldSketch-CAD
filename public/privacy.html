Privacy policy

Draft for review · Prepared 22 October 2026 · Not yet effective. Describes app version 0.1.0 without Sentry.

This policy covers the outlineGo Android app and this website. Data controller & contact.

Sketches and device permissions

Sketches, point coordinates, dimensions, notes and app settings are stored locally on your phone. outlineGo does not upload them to a developer-operated server. No outlineGo account is required.

The camera provides a viewfinder for setting directions. The app does not capture or upload camera photos or video. Motion and orientation sensors support directions and step estimates. Camera and physical activity access are requested through Android permissions, which you can manage in system settings. Denying a permission may limit the corresponding feature.

You can export sketches through Android sharing. Files then go to the destination you select; that destination's privacy practices apply. Exported copies remain outside outlineGo's control. Local technical session records may include sensor readings and sketch identifiers or names; the current app does not send these records to us.

Purchases

Google Play processes Plus purchases under its own terms and privacy policy. The app uses purchase status and purchase verification information to unlock features locally. We do not receive your payment card details through outlineGo. See Google's privacy policy.

Error reporting

This version has no Sentry integration and does not send automatic error reports to Sentry. Error reporting is planned for a future release. This policy and the app's disclosures will be updated before that feature becomes available.

This website and support

This static website does not include analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or embedded third-party fonts. It does not set application cookies. Firebase Hosting, operated by Google, processes connection information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. Google describes retention of Hosting IP addresses as a few months in its Firebase privacy information. Hosting may involve processing outside your country, subject to Google's applicable data processing terms and transfer safeguards.

If you email us, we process your address, message and attachments to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or sketches containing personal data. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries; handling contractual requests and statutory obligations may also require processing. Messages are kept while needed to resolve the request and any related legal obligations or claims, then deleted. Hosting is based on our legitimate interest in delivering a secure website.

Retention and control

You control locally stored sketches through the app and Android. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove them. Exported PNG and DXF files are not a restorable backup of editable app projects. Google controls retention of its purchase records under its policies.

Where applicable under data protection law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data we hold, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Use the email in the contact section below. We cannot retrieve sketches that remain only on your phone. You may complain to your local supervisory authority or Poland's President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes

We will date revisions and provide additional notice when material changes require it. New processing will be disclosed before it starts, with consent where required.
