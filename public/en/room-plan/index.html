Choose how to measure

Direction from your phone.
Length from measurement.

In sensor mode, aim the camera at the next corner and capture the direction. You can estimate length with the step counter: Start → walk → stand still → Stop. For short sides and dimensions needed when buying furniture, check the length with a tape and enter it manually.

The camera is a viewfinder, direction comes from orientation sensors and step-based length is an estimate. Directions and lengths in the example below were entered manually so you can reproduce the outline.

Before the first side

  1. Choose the outline corners and their order.
  2. Save P1. For each side, set the direction before the length.
  3. Check the unit and preview before saving the point.
01

Start with a 5 m wall

Create a Room sketch and save P1. In this example, the first side defines 0°. Enter 5 m, check the preview and save the next point.

The next sides are 4 m and initially 4.8 m. Turn 90° in the same direction at the rectangle’s corners.

02

Correct a dimension without starting over

Open Dimensions and points. Change the third side from 4.8 m to 5 m and save the correction.

Changing a length recalculates subsequent points. Check the outline again; editing is not an additional measurement and does not automatically adjust other errors.

03

Close and check the outline

After three sides, close the sketch to P1. The fourth side is 4 m and is calculated. After correction, the result is an 18 m perimeter and 20 m² area.

Compare the sides with your own measurements. Calculated closure does not replace checking the last wall.
