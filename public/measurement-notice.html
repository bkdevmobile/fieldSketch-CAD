Draft · Prepared 22 October 2026 · Proposed wording for the app's first-start notice.

outlineGo is designed for non-professional measurements and everyday sketches.

  • Phone sensors and manual input can introduce errors. Step-based lengths are estimates and depend on your step length and how you move.
  • Verify important dimensions with suitable equipment. Do not rely on the app alone for surveying, construction, property boundaries or safety-critical decisions.
  • Sketches are stored on your phone. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove them. PNG and DXF exports cannot restore editable sketches.
  • Stay aware of your surroundings while measuring.

Proposed acknowledgement: “I understand that measurements are approximate and that important dimensions must be verified.”

The acknowledgement confirms understanding of these limitations. It is not consent to error reporting and does not waive consumer rights. This website does not record an acknowledgement.
