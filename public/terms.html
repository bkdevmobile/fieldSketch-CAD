Purpose and measurement limitations

outlineGo is an Android tool for approximate, non-professional measurements and dimensioned 2D sketches. Record directions and lengths, review and edit the outline, add notes and export output files. It is intended for everyday planning and reference. It is not a surveying instrument and does not provide certified measurements, establish property boundaries or automatically scan a space into a verified plan.

Directions can be relative, magnetic or entered manually, depending on device capabilities. Lengths can be entered in metres, centimetres, feet or inches, or estimated from steps using a configurable step length. A camera view helps you aim; it does not determine distances from an image. The interface supports Polish, English, German, Spanish and French, and light, dark or system themes. Choosing a language does not change measurement units.

Sensor drift, magnetic interference, phone movement, device capabilities, step length and user input can affect results. Step-based lengths are estimates. No numerical accuracy level is promised. Do not use the app as the sole basis for surveying, construction, structural decisions, property boundaries, safety-critical work or tasks requiring verified precision. Check important dimensions with suitable equipment. See the measurement notice.

A closing edge may be calculated rather than independently measured. An area is available only for a valid closed outline. Neither output certifies the accuracy of the original measurements.

An independent return measurement, where recorded, does not automatically adjust the outline or distribute measurement error. A calculated closing edge is identified separately from a measured side. Displayed decimal places, angle snapping and manual corrections do not establish measurement accuracy; review the selected units and inputs before use.

Availability and compatibility

Distribution depends on Google Play country and device availability, compatibility and applicable restrictions. These terms do not promise worldwide availability or support for every device. Hardware capabilities affect camera, sensor and step-counting features; manual entry remains available.

DXF is intended for compatible CAD software. Appearance, text and units can vary between programs. Check imported geometry and units before further work. Exporting or opening a file does not verify the underlying measurements.

The current DXF export is a 2D AutoCAD 2000 ASCII DXF (AC1015) in a local XY coordinate system, using the selected length unit. Outline geometry, point labels, length labels and notes are placed on separate layers. Length labels are text, not associative CAD dimensions, and may need manual updates after editing geometry in another program. The app does not provide DWG export, georeferenced surveying coordinates or an editable-project import or restore function for exported PNG/DXF files.
