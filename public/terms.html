Purpose and limitations

outlineGo is an Android tool for making approximate, non-professional sketches from directions and lengths. It is intended for everyday planning and reference. It is not a surveying instrument and does not provide certified measurements.

Sensor drift, magnetic interference, phone movement, step length and user input can affect results. No numerical accuracy level is promised. Do not use the app as the sole basis for property boundaries, construction, structural decisions, safety-critical work or other tasks requiring verified precision. Verify important dimensions with suitable equipment. See the measurement notice.

Use and local data

You may install and use the app for its intended purpose, subject to these terms and applicable Google Play terms. You retain your rights in your sketches and exported content. Use the app lawfully and respect others' rights.

The app stores sketches locally without an outlineGo account or cloud synchronisation. Deleting a sketch, clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove it. PNG and DXF exports are output files, not a backup that restores editable sketches in the app. Review exports and keep copies where needed.

Free and Plus

Free supports up to 3 saved sketches and 8 points per sketch, including the first point. Its PNG exports include a watermark. Notes and corrections are included.

Plus removes these product limits, removes the PNG watermark and enables DXF export. Device storage and performance constraints still apply. Plus is a one-time Google Play in-app purchase, not a subscription. The store presents the price, applicable taxes and availability before you buy.

Purchase restoration uses Google Play and the Google account that made the purchase. Restoring Plus restores the entitlement; it does not restore local sketches or transfer them between devices. Internet access and Google Play services may be needed for purchases and restoration.
