Aim at the next corner, capture direction, start the step counter, stand still and stop, then save the next point. See how outlineGo turns a direction and an estimated length into a 2D sketch.

Emulator demonstration with simulated direction and step readings. The virtual camera uses an AI-generated garden image. The app interface, length calculation, saving and PNG export are real. This is not a recording of physical measurement or sensor accuracy. The camera is a viewfinder; step-based lengths are estimates.
