outlineGo — your first sketch

Music: Carefree — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) · CC BY 4.0. Edited, repeated, volume adjusted and faded. Credits.

  1. New sketch
    Create “Patio” in manual mode. Example: a rectangle measuring 4 × 3 m. Directions and lengths are entered manually.
  2. First side
    Save P1 and set the first direction to 0°. Enter 4 m, review the side and save P2. Follow the same three steps for each side.
  3. Next sides
    Enter a 90° turn to the left. Add 3 m and 3.8 m, saving each point. After the third side, choose Leave open.
  4. Correction
    Choose Edit last side in the summary. Change 3.8 m to 4 m and save changes. The correction updates the saved sketch.
  5. Close outline
    Continue → Finish → Close outline. The side to P1 is calculated, not measured. Perimeter 14 m · area 12 m².
  6. Export PNG
    Choose Export PNG → Share PNG. Free keeps the outlineGo watermark. Your PNG is ready to save or share.
