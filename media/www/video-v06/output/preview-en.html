outlineGo — CAMERA AND STEP COUNTER

Emulator demonstration with simulated direction and step sensor readings. A garden image file supplies the virtual camera. The interface, length conversion, sketch persistence and exported PNG are real. This film does not document a physical measurement or sensor accuracy.

Music: Montauk Point — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) · CC BY 4.0. Excerpt, volume adjusted and faded. Credits.

  1. Aim the camera
    Aim the crosshair at the next corner. Turn the phone to aim.
  2. Save the direction
    Wait for a stable direction reading. Then continue to measure the length.
  3. Step counter Start / Stop
    Start the step counter at the first corner. Walk to the next corner, stand still and press Stop.
  4. Save the point
    Check the step count and length. Each saved point extends the sketch.
  5. Next direction
    Turn the phone to aim at the next corner. Save the direction before walking.
  6. Next side
    Start → walk → stand still → Stop. Review the result and save the next point.
  7. Finished sketch
    Close the outline and check the result. The side to P1 is calculated.
