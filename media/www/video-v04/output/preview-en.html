outlineGo — FROM SKETCH TO CAD

Music: Life of Riley — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) · CC BY 4.0. Excerpt, volume adjusted and faded. Credits.

  1. Export DXF
    Choose Export DXF in the summary. DXF export is available with Plus.
  2. Open the file
    Transfer or copy the DXF to your computer. Open the exported file in LibreCAD.
  3. Same outline
    Fit the view to see the entire sketch. Check the points and length labels.
  4. Units
    Open the current drawing preferences. In the Units tab, check: Meter.
  5. Check the scale
    Select only the outline, without length labels. Total Length should read 14 m.
