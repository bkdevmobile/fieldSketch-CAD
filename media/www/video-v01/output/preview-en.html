  1. Sketch
    A 4 × 3 m patio. Perimeter 14 m. Area 12 m². From manually entered lengths to a file.
  2. Save a side
    Enter direction and length manually. Review the side before saving the point.
  3. Export PNG
    Save or share your PNG. The Free version keeps the watermark.
  4. Export DXF
    Choose Export DXF in the summary. DXF export is available with Plus.
  5. Continue in CAD
    Open the exported DXF in LibreCAD. Check the outline, labels and drawing unit.
  6. Get the app
    Sketch, export and keep working. Get outlineGo on Google Play.
