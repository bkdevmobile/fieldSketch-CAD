outlineGo — SKETCH A ROOM

The room measurement scene is an AI-generated illustration with a gentle zoom out. App screens and the exported PNG are real. This film does not document a physical measurement or automatic phone measurement.

Music: Dream Culture — Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) · CC BY 4.0. Excerpt, volume adjusted and faded. Credits.

  1. New sketch
    Create “Room” in manual mode. Example: a room measuring 5 × 4 m.
  2. First side
    Set the direction and enter the length. Review the side before saving the point.
  3. Next sides
    Add 4 m, then 4.8 m. Save each side as the next point.
  4. Correction
    Choose Edit last side. Change 4.8 m to 5 m and save changes.
  5. Close outline
    Choose Finish → Close outline. The side to P1 is calculated.
  6. Export PNG
    Save or share your PNG. The Free version keeps the watermark.
